Toward a Scientometric Research Paper in Your Chosen Field
Course Facilitator: Dr. Ryan Manuel D. Guido

Purpose of this Questionnaire

This questionnaire is a course requirement in SCIEED261 – Advanced Environmental Science under Dr. Ryan Manuel D. Guido. It builds your research profile — your background, skills, interests, and goals — so that you can select a field within (or allied to) environmental science and develop a scientometric (bibliometric) research paper on it.

It takes about 20–25 minutes to complete. Items marked * are required. Please answer honestly; there are no right or wrong answers, and your responses will not affect your grade except as evidence of completion.

Data Privacy Notice and Consent

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC), please read the following before proceeding.

  1. Personal Information Controller. The data collected through this form are controlled by the course facilitator, Dr. Ryan Manuel D. Guido, for SCIEED261 – Advanced Environmental Science.
  2. Data collected. Your name, student number, email address, academic program, educational and professional background, research experience, skills, and research interests. No sensitive personal information (e.g., health, religion, political affiliation, government-issued ID numbers) is requested.
  3. Purpose. The data will be used solely to (a) build your graduate research profile, (b) advise you on selecting a field and topic for a scientometric paper, (c) plan course instruction and consultations, and (d) produce aggregated, de-identified summaries for academic and course-improvement purposes.
  4. Voluntary participation. Answering this questionnaire is voluntary. You may skip non-required items and may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the course facilitator, without penalty.
  5. Storage and security. Responses are transmitted securely (HTTPS) and stored through the Formspree form service and the facilitator's password-protected accounts. Access is limited to the course facilitator. Reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards are applied.
  6. Sharing and disclosure. Your personal data will not be sold or shared with third parties. Any report, presentation, or publication derived from this questionnaire will present data only in aggregated or anonymized form, so that no individual respondent can be identified.
  7. Retention. Data will be kept only for the duration of the course and for one (1) academic year thereafter for advising and documentation, after which they will be securely deleted, unless you consent to longer retention.
  8. Your rights. As a data subject, you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to rectification, to data portability, to damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).
  9. Contact. For questions, corrections, or withdrawal of consent, contact the course facilitator through the official course channel or your institution's Data Protection Officer.
